New features

The pertussis-specific capabilities introduced in 1.0.

Modeling

Eight-compartment SEIRS with a partial-immunity track

Pertussis is modeled as a naive track (S→E→I→R) running alongside a partially-immune track (Sₚ→Eₚ→Iₚ→Rₚ) — capturing the milder, less-infectious “silent reservoir” that keeps whooping cough circulating in highly vaccinated populations.

  • Two-source force of infection β(I + σIₚ); partial susceptibles infected at reduced rate δ
  • Separate naive and partial infectious periods — faster recovery when partially immune
  • Three waning pathways: natural full→partial (ω₁), partial→susceptible (ω₂), and vaccine→susceptible (ω₃)
  • R₀-calibrated transmission on the geography’s age-structured contact matrix
Vaccination

Age-stratified vaccination and the DTaP/Tdap schedule

Vaccination moves susceptibles into partial immunity (S→Sₚ). A one-click CDC DTaP/Tdap preset and a dedicated Vaccination Planner let you set coverage per age band and test “what-if” strategies — all of it persistent between sessions.

  • CDC DTaP/Tdap schedule preset mapped onto the model age bands
  • Age-stratified campaign builder — per-band coverage, efficacy and timing
  • Saved campaign sets and coverage-by-age profiles that persist across sessions (JSON-backed)
  • Age-stratified starting immunity applied directly from a saved coverage profile
Interventions

Contact-intervention planner — NPIs by setting

Plan non-pharmaceutical interventions as time-bounded contact reductions within a single setting (home, school, work, or community) or across all of them. Because mixing is age-specific, a school closure principally reduces contact among school-age bands — the same bands that dominate pertussis transmission.

  • Reduce contacts by a chosen percentage over a start–end day window
  • Target one layer (home / school / work / community) or all layers at once
  • Stack multiple interventions; each is scaled into the age-structured contact matrix
  • Combine with vaccination campaigns to compare mitigation strategies side by side
Calibration

Local calibration to Lane County case data

Load observed cases cross-tabulated by age and vaccination-up-to-date status. The status maps onto the model’s tracks — not up-to-date → naive (I), up-to-date → partial (Iₚ) — giving a structural target the partial-immunity model is uniquely able to reproduce.

  • Observed dataset with No→naive / Yes→partial track mapping
  • Overlay & compare, seed the outbreak from observed counts, or coarse auto-calibrate (R₀, σ, Sₚ share)
  • Vaccinated-share-of-cases and age-distribution fit metrics, with the case-share-vs-coverage caveat surfaced
Visualization

Observed-vs-modeled and age-mixing views

New result views make the pertussis dynamics legible — who is getting infected, who is transmitting, and how the model compares to reality.

  • Observed vs modeled tab: case age distribution, vaccinated share by band, and fit RMSE
  • Contacts-by-setting: contacts per person across home / school / work / community
  • Summary metrics now count the partial track (Iₚ, Eₚ→Iₚ) so the reservoir isn’t undercounted
Initial conditions

Endemic immunity-landscape controls

Start a run from a realistic endemic state rather than a naïve population, splitting infection and immunity across the two tracks by age.

  • Partial-track infection fraction and the Sₚ share of the immune pool
  • Age-stratified starting immunity from a saved coverage profile (Lane County seeded)
Updated

Updates

Platform and reliability improvements shipped alongside the model.

Stability
Single-threaded native math + auto-restart launcher. Forces single-threaded numpy/BLAS/numexpr and adds a supervised launcher (run_dashboard.ps1 / .sh) that restarts the app automatically if it exits.
Vaccination
Vaccination-rate hardening. An empty susceptible age band no longer produces NaN; the rate is zero when there is no one to vaccinate.
Metrics
Partial track included. Peak prevalence and attack rate now include Iₚ and Eₚ→Iₚ, so the partially-immune reservoir is counted.
Branding
IRS / SOAR attribution. International Responder Systems footer logo and the EpiStorm / ISI Foundation credit line, on every page.
Seasonality
“Low” option added. Restores the missing low-seasonality setting and fixes a latent crash it caused in the influenza preset.
